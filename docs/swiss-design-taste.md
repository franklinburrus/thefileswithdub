# Frank's Design Taste — Swiss

**Recorded 2026-09-21 (his words: "my design style is what people call Swiss
design language").** His taste is the Swiss / International Typographic
Style: Helvetica, minimalism, uniformity, grid discipline, clean aesthetic.

## The rules

- Brand-book fonts are frozen — "it is what it is." The Swiss taste governs
  everything non-font: layout, spacing, alignment, restraint.
- Designer-eye leverage: Gilbert has standing authority to make designer-eye
  calls WITHOUT asking Frank. Reject burned-in broadcast UI (status bars,
  player chrome, progress bars, overlays), broken or cluttered elements,
  anything no working designer would ship. When in doubt, choose the cleaner
  option.
- No-repeat rule: a brand/design directive is said ONCE. If a room misreads
  it, stop, verify the interpretation against the law, and re-brief — never
  make Frank repeat himself. (The logo/badge took four conversations on
  2026-09-21; that must not recur.)
