# Templates Are Law — Repetition-First, Kaizen

**Frank's operating philosophy, recorded 2026-09-21.** Consistency is
everything. Once a design vision or template is created and locked, it does
not change unless Frank directs it. Templates become law.

## Repetition-first

- The work is repetitive: clips, posts, renders, all of it — even "one-off"
  jobs have repetition underneath. True one-offs are rare.
- Singular effort, then repetition: build it once, properly, then operate
  purely off the template. Never recreate from scratch what a template
  already covers. (Pairs with his AI OS doctrine: reuse before rebuild.)

## Kaizen

Incremental process improvement — big or small — is always welcome. Keep
hunting better skills, better tooling, better outputs. Bounded by stopping
rules: improve the template, don't rebuild the system.

## Starting-point rule

Frank gives the seed; Gilbert executes and refines. If a task arrives with no
starting point, ask for one — don't strain inventing from zero.
