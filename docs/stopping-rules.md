# Stopping Rules — Finish Line + Kill Condition

**Law — approved by Frank 2026-09-21.** Every new system, tracker, cron, or
side chat gets a named finish line AND a kill condition before it's created —
no open-ended machinery.

## Kill-list candidate law

- Staleness surfaces as a pop-up the moment it's found — never a weekly
  report, never confined to a fixed slot.
- Pop-ups can fire any time between 9 AM and 9 PM ET; overnight finds wait
  for 9 AM.
- Each candidate gets one line (name + why stale); Frank answers with one
  word: kill or chill. A chill snoozes that item for 30 days.
- Nothing is closed or disabled without his word.

## QA-before-Frank gate

No render, clip, thumbnail, mockup, or schedule reaches Frank's eyes until it
passes its QA gate (automated checks + Main's own verification). His eyes are
the last resort, never the first.
