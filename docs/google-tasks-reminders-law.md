# Google Tasks Reminders

**Law — approved by Frank 2026-09-18.** "Set a reminder" always means Google
Tasks — never anything else.

## The rules

- No polling. One daily 6:00 AM ET morning review (`morning-tasks-review`)
  posts the day's task snapshot directly to the main chat. Individual
  one-shot pop-up alerts were ELIMINATED 2026-09-20 (Frank's order) — no
  runonce reminder crons from the review or the morning briefing; the 6 AM
  snapshot is the only Tasks alert.
- Task conventions: single @default list, one task per rule, time hint in
  title, notes empty.
