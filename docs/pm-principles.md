# PM Standing Principles

**Standing law — confirmed by Frank 2026-09-23.** The eleven project-schema elements are the Project Manager's operating doctrine.

## The eleven

1. **End-state expectation** — the PM measures every lane's status against it on the morning pull; "progress" only counts if it moves toward done.
2. **Quality expectation** — the Media Manager grades every PASS/FAIL against it; it's the written bar, not a vibe.
3. **Roadmap** — the PM drives milestones in order; anything off-sequence or stalled gets flagged.
4. **Gates** — the Media Manager enforces the sequence (Captions → QA → Main → Frank). Nothing skips a gate.
5. **QA** — who verifies, against what, with what evidence. The Media Manager runs it; self-declared passes don't exist.
6. **Drift management** — the PM ages every project's last-movement date; 48 hours of silence with no named reason fires the kill/chill pop-up automatically.
7. **Finish line** — the PM closes projects that hit it instead of leaving them running warm.
8. **Kill condition** — the PM watches for triggers and surfaces candidates the moment they appear, any time 9 AM–9 PM.
9. **Definition of Done** — checkable, no interpretation. Completion claims get verified against it, never trusted on a room's word.
10. **Outcome owner** — one accountable person per project; the PM routes stalls and escalations to them first.
11. **Waiting on** — the dependency map; the PM hunts stragglers blocking downstream work and escalates blockers on the 24-hour clock.

## How the day runs

- The PM's morning pass runs all eleven against every active project. Every active project section in the project map names all eleven — a missing field is a defect, not a style choice.
- The Media Manager wields 2, 4, 5, and 9 as the wall.
- Nothing reaches Frank except through Main.

## Adopted learnings (Ledger/Notion audit, 2026-09-22)

- Every law gets ONE canonical text home (this docs/ folder); everything else carries pointers plus the what-and-why.
- The project map must live in a real layer — machine-only stores are a serious risk.
- Layer discipline: Notion = explanations, Sheets = structured records, Ledger = learning index, Drive = doctrine/sources. Operational records don't live in Notion.
- Kill stale and duplicates on sight: frozen tabs, dead rules, orphan pages, blank duplicates.
