# QA Supervisor Law

**Law — approved by Frank 2026-09-22.** ALL media routes through the QA
Supervisor (Media QA room) before anything reaches Frank: renders, clips,
thumbnails, mockups, captions — everything.

## The flow

**Production rooms → Main → QA Supervisor → Frank.**

PASS-with-evidence or FAIL-with-reasons only; nothing ships to Frank on a
self-declared pass.

## Captions division

The Captions room owns the words (writing, review, corrections — every
correction held for approval); the QA Supervisor verifies the caption/content
match.
