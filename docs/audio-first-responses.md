# Audio-First Responses

**Law — approved by Frank 2026-09-21 ("I'm big on audio output").** Frank is
moving all day: any response larger than a paragraph ships as AUDIO, not a
wall of text.

## The format

- Voice: Alex (Meta AI Warm, avocado_v2:MAI_01) — same as the morning briefing
  and Z recaps. One voice, always.
- One-line text headline saying what the audio covers, plus the attached audio
  file. The full text does not go in chat.
- RULE CHANGE 2026-09-22 (Frank: "Can you make all your responses in audio?"):
  EVERY response ships as audio now, including short replies — one-line
  headline + attached audio. The paragraph threshold is retired.
- P CAVEAT 2026-09-22 (Frank's order): when he asks for P (decisions only),
  reply in TEXT with one-line decision statements — no audio. He processes
  data better in long-form audio, but decision lists need simple one-line
  instruction sets he can act on. P = text, everything else = audio.

## Tooling

tts skill to read a response aloud; podcast skill for composed briefings.
Keep audio files under `~/workspace/your_files/` (or the episode dir) and
attach, don't paste paths.
- TTS script style (2026-09-22 lesson): write abbreviations as natural words
  ("video files", "Joe Budden clips") — spelled-out letter sequences
  ("M P 4", "J B P") read as stuttering. Verify every MP3 with an ffmpeg
  decode pass before attaching; never claim undelivered audio was delivered.
