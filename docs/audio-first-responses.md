# Audio-First Responses

**Law — approved by Frank 2026-09-21 ("I'm big on audio output").** Frank is
moving all day: any response larger than a paragraph ships as AUDIO, not a
wall of text.

## The format

- Voice: Alex (Meta AI Warm, avocado_v2:MAI_01) — same as the morning briefing
  and Z recaps. One voice, always.
- One-line text headline saying what the audio covers, plus the attached audio
  file. The full text does not go in chat.
- Short replies (a paragraph or less) stay text — that's the threshold.

## Tooling

tts skill to read a response aloud; podcast skill for composed briefings.
Keep audio files under `~/workspace/your_files/` (or the episode dir) and
attach, don't paste paths.

**Canonical source — supersedes copies elsewhere.**
